# Infogrid Sprint — other Mac

1. Copy this whole `sid` folder to the other Mac (AirDrop, USB, iCloud, Git).
2. Open the folder in Cursor.
3. Open `ONE-PROMPT.txt`, copy everything under the line, paste into Cursor chat, send.
4. Follow the agent (Firebase free + Netlify Drop free).
5. Bookmark the Netlify URL on both phones/laptops.

After `cloud-config.json` is in the folder and deployed, new devices auto-connect.
